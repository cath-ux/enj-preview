For the record

Excavating New Jersey LLC is an owner-operated excavation and septic contractor based in Wantage, serving Sussex and northern Morris County for nearly 20 years. We design, permit and install septic systems to NJDEP Chapter 199 (N.J.A.C. 7:9A), and handle land clearing, drainage, sewer work and site excavation on rocky, high-water-table ground.

Why people call us back
We would rather tell you it is a repair than sell you a system you do not need.

Most people who call us have never bought a septic system and hope never to buy another. That imbalance is exactly where the industry gets its reputation, so we run the opposite way.

That one person is Mike White, the owner. You get a free site evaluation, a written estimate you can hand to a lender or an attorney, and Mike accountable from the first walk-through to the final health department sign-off.

If the honest answer is that a repair will hold, that is the answer you get.

The ground we work

Sussex County ground decides the design, so we read it first

Shallow bedrock

Rock sits close to the surface across much of the county. We design around it before the permit is filed, not after the dig starts.

Seasonal high water

A lot that drains in August can be saturated in April. New Jersey code measures for the wet season, and so do we.

Glacial till

Mixed soils change across a single yard. Two test pits often tell two different stories, which is why we dig both.

Selling a house

Failed the inspection with a closing date on the line?

This is the call we take most often. The septic is rarely the hard part. The hard part is not knowing the number or the timeline while everyone else in the sale waits on you.

We can usually tell you after one visit whether your date is realistic, and we will say so plainly if it is not.

  • Free evaluation and a firm written estimate
  • Permits and health department chased by us

Asked on almost every call

Answered the way we would answer on the phone

What does a new septic system cost?

It depends on the soil, the slope and what the code requires for your lot, which is why we do not quote before seeing the site. The evaluation and the written estimate are free, and the figure we give you is the figure we hold to.

How long does the whole process take?

The health department usually sets the pace rather than the construction. After a site visit we can tell you realistically what to expect for your township.

Can I pay at closing?

In many home-sale situations, yes. We will walk through how that works for your sale and what your attorney or lender needs from us.

Do I really need a full replacement?

Not always. A camera inspection and a look at the field often show that a targeted repair will do, and we will tell you when that is the case.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, and there is a NAWT-certified septic inspector on staff. We are happy to send documentation before you commit to anything.

My property is solid rock. Can you still do it?

Yes. We own heavy-duty hydraulic breakers for exactly this ground, from Smoke Rise granite to the iron-heavy rock around Rockaway Township. Rock changes the plan and the price, and the written estimate spells that out before we start.

Do you offer green or advanced treatment septic systems?

Yes. We install advanced treatment systems, including Singulair Green units, where lots are tight or environmentally sensitive.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes. We build commercial septic and site work across Sussex and northern Morris County, from Route 23 businesses to larger developments, with engineering and TWA permitting handled in house.
